Artifact 4 Reflection

For my fourth portfolio artifact, I selected a recent assignment that demonstrates a different dimension of my AI and machine learning competencies. Unlike my previous artifacts—which focused on model implementation, data analysis, and applied problem-solving—this artifact showcases my ability to communicate AI/ML concepts clearly, evaluate technical work critically, and adapt academic material for a professional audience. I chose this piece because it highlights both technical understanding and reflective analysis, which are essential skills in the AI/ML field. In preparing this artifact for my portfolio, I revised the original submission to make it more accessible to viewers who may not have deep technical backgrounds. This included reorganizing the content for clarity, expanding explanations of key AI/ML techniques, and refining the visuals and language to match the standards of a professional portfolio. These improvements ensure that instructors, employers, or industry professionals can quickly understand the purpose of the work, the AI/ML concepts involved, and the value of the insights gained. This artifact also reflects my ability to tailor technical material for different audiences—an important competency emphasized throughout the course. By revising the artifact, I demonstrated not only domain knowledge but also the communication and presentation skills that are vital in real-world AI/ML environments. The final version highlights my capacity for critical evaluation, my understanding of model behavior, and my commitment to producing polished, reader-friendly work. With this artifact added to my portfolio, alongside artifacts 1–3, my collection now presents a well-rounded representation of my progress in AI and machine learning. It demonstrates my growth in areas such as modeling, analysis, communication, adaptation, and professional presentation, strengthening the overall impact of my portfolio. his artifact shows potential employers and academic reviewers that I can work with real datasets, make data-driven decisions, and build predictive models aligned with business or research goals. It highlights my growth as a developing Machine Learning practitioner and establishes a foundation for more advanced work in AI.
